# Chinese trophy text references

See the comprehensive [Trophy documentation](TROPHIES.md) for full multi-language provenance,
official PSN sources, and artwork architecture details.

The Chinese (`zh`) trophy names and descriptions follow the Simplified Chinese PSN trophy wording
reproduced for Bloodborne's `NPWR05818_00` list on [PSNine](https://www.psnine.com/psngame/5818).
All 40 trophy names, descriptions, punctuation, and line breaks match the official PlayStation Network
release.
