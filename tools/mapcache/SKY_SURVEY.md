# Sky / moon / sun survey (Bloodborne 1.09, read-only)

Generated by `sky_survey.py` (tables below come from its output). Every `map\mapstudio\*.msb.dcx` (24 files) plus all chalice MSBs in `map\mapstudio\m29_*\` were read.
A "sky part" is a MapPiece, Object or DummyObject whose model has a mesh with `sky`, `moon` or `sun` in its MTD or material name.
Positions are world space (part transform applied, same convention as `mapcache.py`). Distances and angular sizes are measured from the map centre, which is the median of the non-sky map-piece mesh centres. Classes are heuristic: dome = surrounds the centre and is tall; cloud band = wide ring or strip; moon/sun = named moon/sun or small and far.

## What the `_01` / `_11` variants are

These are gameplay variants of the same map, not sky variants. They use the base map's model folder and its event script; none has its own `.emevd`.
- m24_00_00_01 vs _00: 3 added parts (c0000_0028, h005501_0000, h005502_0000) and 2 collision group changes.
- m24_01_00_11 vs _00: 9 ConnectCollision parts become type 9, plus a few enemy and map-piece group changes.
- All other `_01` files likewise contain the same sky parts, models, positions, entity ids and groups as their base map.

## (2) How several skies of one map are told apart

They are not told apart by the MSB file, and they are not told apart by draw groups.
- All skies of one map sit in the same MSB, usually at the same position.
- Their draw/display groups (`groups@0x4C`, `groups@0x6C`) are identical across the time-of-day variants.

They are told apart by the event script `event\<map>.emevd.dcx`:
- One event per map reads the world-phase flags **9800 / 9801 / 9802** (evening, night, blood moon). It uses `1003[101]` (goto label if event flag) to pick a branch.
- Each branch enables exactly one sky part and disables the others by the part's **EntityID** (MSB part entity data, the first int at the offset in part +0xB0).
- Map pieces are switched with `2012[01]` (args: entity id, state). Objects are switched with `2005[03]` (args: entity id, state).
- The default sky is the one enabled when no flag is set: Cathedral Ward m900000 "daytime", Central m24_00 m900010.
